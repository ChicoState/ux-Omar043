## My Experience with the Honeywell Home System
# My Goal
During one of the hottest periods of the summer, I had wished to change the temperature of the house. I found out that the Honeywell Home was the thermostat of my apartment building and that I had to work with it to get the place at my desired temperature

# Attempt at accomplishing my goal
In order to get the house at my desired temperature, I had first changed the temperature setting. I saw that the set temperature and the room temperature were only different by a couple of degrees, so I changed the temperature to one even lower. After the temperature had not changed after 5 minutes, I looked up a youtube video on how to change the temperature and found out that I had to change a fan setting. After changing the fan setting, the temperature lowered to the one that I set.

# Notes on attempt / Improvements
I felt that there were some elements in the thermostat that I was not made aware that should be used, but changing the temperature was straightforward with the plus and minus buttons. A device like the thermostat was probably made with the homeowner having the user manual in possession, but overall, the controls were easy to pick up.
